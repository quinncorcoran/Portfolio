A new color that came out of the comments

I handle every comment and DM on Seymour's social accounts, and I treat them as research. Listening to what customers were asking for, I pitched a new color for one of our most popular paints: Stainless-Blast Black.

In 2025, its first full year on our website, it sold 4,200 cans against 2,700 for the original color. The original still grew from 2,400 the year before, so the new color added sales instead of moving them.

About

I'm a social media manager and video creator in Chicago with six years of making short-form video for brands. Since 2023 I've run social at Seymour of Sycamore: organic content, creator partnerships, paid media direction, and quarterly reporting to our Marketing Director and CFO. I manage one direct report and mentor our marketing interns.

I started in journalism. At the University of Montana I led the video team at the Montana Kaimin and won a national SPJ Mark of Excellence Award for an enrollment investigation. I was also an editor and photographer on Trash Talk, a Montana PBS student documentary named a national winner for in-depth television reporting. That training is why I start with the story and then check it against the numbers.

Tools: Premiere Pro, After Effects, CapCut, Photoshop, Lightroom, Figma, Meta Business Suite, Sprout Social, Emplifi, Sprinklr, Brandwatch, Google Analytics. AI video with Firefly, Kling and Runway, blended with real footage.
